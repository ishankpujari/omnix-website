What it does

Like a librarian and a careful cleaner for your Mac.

🔎

Find anything by describing it, in any words

“The contract I signed with my landlord last year.” “Papers that let me work in the US” finds your work permit, even though it never says those words. Omnix understands what documents mean, plus dates, places, types and sizes, and shows why each result matched. Files you open come first next time.

Your disk as rings, coloured by what’s safe to clean, by type or by age. It points out what to look at first, and shows the result before you clean.

Space you can safely take back

Caches, build folders, duplicates, deleted apps’ leftovers and blurry photos, plus ways to keep files and use less space: offload to iCloud, shrink videos, zip old projects. Each says why it’s safe; everything can be undone.

🛡️

“Is it safe to delete?”

For anything on your Mac: can it be rebuilt, is your code backed up, is there a copy, does it look important. Then a clear verdict.

🗂️

Organize and clean by asking

“Put my tax files in one folder.” “Delete my 2023 screenshots.” “Find my lease and tell me when it ends.” You see the plan first, and every change can be undone. New downloads get a suggested folder too.

Quick search with ⌥ Space from any app, a menu bar icon with free space, Finder right-click, and Siri Shortcuts.

Privacy

Your files never leave your Mac.

Most AI tools send your documents to a server to read them. Omnix doesn’t. It has no account, never uploads anything, and does all of its reading and thinking on your Mac. Its only internet connection is checking for updates, which you can turn off.

Read the full privacy policy →
  • On-device AI only. Omnix’s own AI models (for meaning and pictures) are built into the app, and answers can use Apple’s on-device model. All of it runs on your Mac. Not ChatGPT, not any cloud AI.
  • Encrypted on your Mac. What it saves for fast search is encrypted with a key in your Keychain, with sensitive information hidden.
  • You’re in control. Every move or delete shows a plan first, goes to the Trash, and can be undone. Photos, Notes and Mail are off until you turn them on.
  • No tracking. No analytics, no ads, no automatic crash reports. This website has no trackers either.

Install

Get the beta.

Free while in beta. Needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later). Answers from inside documents need Apple Intelligence turned on; everything else works without it.

Download Omnix.dmg

Version 0.1.0 beta

  1. Open the downloaded file and drag Omnix into Applications.
  2. Open Omnix. Because this beta isn’t notarized by Apple yet, macOS stops it the first time. Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway, and confirm.
  3. Click Allow when macOS asks about your Documents, Desktop, Downloads and iCloud Drive. That’s what lets Omnix search them.
  4. Look around. Omnix opens on Storage, with the space map. While it reads your files (a few minutes the first time), try Ask: “find my lease” or “when does my passport expire?”.

Beta

Help shape Omnix.

When an answer is wrong, tap Wrong answer? under it. Reports stay on your Mac until you choose to export them in Settings and send them. If something breaks, Settings › Create problem report writes a short report (no file names) for you to send.

FAQ

Questions.

Is it free?

Yes, while it’s in beta.

Does Omnix use ChatGPT or send my files anywhere?

No. It uses only Apple’s on-device model, which runs on your Mac. Omnix never sends your files or questions anywhere and has no account. Its only internet connection is checking for updates, which you can turn off. Search, safety checks and cleanup follow fixed rules and work even without Apple Intelligence.

What does it save, and where?

The text it has read, what your files mean, your chat, Clean up’s history and your undo history, in its own folder on your Mac (~/Library/Application Support/com.omnix.desktop), encrypted, with sensitive numbers hidden. You can delete any of it in Settings.

How do updates work?

Once a day Omnix checks whether a newer version is out. If there is one, it shows “Update now”, and one click downloads, installs and restarts it. Your search, settings and history stay. The check sends nothing about you or your files, and you can turn it off in Settings.

Why is the download so big?

About 700 MB, because the AI that understands your documents and pictures is built into the app instead of running on a server. That’s what lets it work offline and keep your files private.

Does it search Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive?

Yes, if you use their Mac apps: those apps keep a folder on your Mac, and Omnix searches it like any other folder. Files kept online only are found by name; Omnix never downloads them. Omnix doesn’t sign in to these services.

Can it clean up my Gmail or Google Photos?

No. Those live on Google’s servers, and Omnix never connects to them. To see what fills your Google storage, use Google’s own page at one.google.com/storage.

Does it look at my Photos library or Notes?

Only if you turn them on in Settings, and macOS asks for your permission first. Omnix never changes your notes; anything it removes from Photos goes to Recently Deleted for 30 days.

Can it delete my files by mistake?

It never deletes on its own. Every change shows a plan first and waits for your click. Deleting means moving to the Trash, where you can put it back, and most changes have Undo.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

Not in this beta. It needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later).

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

No. It makes some answers smarter (reading answers out of documents, custom checklists, planning long requests and chat). Search by meaning, pictures, expiry dates, totals, cleanup and organizing all work without it.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit Omnix, drag it from Applications to the Trash, and delete the folder ~/Library/Application Support/com.omnix.desktop. If you added it to Finder, also delete ~/Library/Services/Ask Omnix.workflow (or click Remove from Finder in Settings first).
